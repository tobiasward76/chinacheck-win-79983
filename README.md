## About ChinaCheck

ChinaCheck is designed for European hospitality operators that want a clearer view of how prepared they are for guests from China. It brings together location-based demand signals and practical readiness checks so teams can compare opportunities across markets and identify where improvements may matter most.

The tool is especially useful for owners, managers, and commercial teams who need a quick way to evaluate channel coverage, payment support, language accessibility, and digital touchpoints. By turning those signals into a structured readiness view, ChinaCheck helps users focus on the operational changes that could improve appeal to Chinese travelers.

## Features

- Assesses Chinese traveler demand by location
- Reviews readiness for Chinese guests across core service areas
- Highlights gaps in channel coverage, payment support, language, and digital accessibility
- Estimates potential revenue upside from improving China readiness
- Supports hospitality planning and market prioritization
- Helps teams identify operational weaknesses before expanding outreach
- Presents readiness insights in a web-based workflow
- Useful for commercial, operations, and guest-experience planning
